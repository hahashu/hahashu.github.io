I am a Data Scientist at Microsoft UK, working on applied AI. I'm interested in AI for social good, modalities beyond vision and sound, and domain-specific Human-Centred AI — bringing domain expertise into natural Human-AI interaction. I code 👩🏻‍💻 and build prototypes.

I recently completed my PhD at UCL [Computer Science](https://www.ucl.ac.uk/computer-science/research/research-labs/multi-sensory-devices) and [Interaction Centre (UCLIC)](https://www.ucl.ac.uk/uclic) (Dr. soon 🎓), with my thesis *Perceptual Alignment in Human-Centred AI*, supervised by [Marianna Obrist](https://profiles.ucl.ac.uk/78441-marianna-obrist), [Elia Gatti](https://profiles.ucl.ac.uk/93101-elia-gatti) and [Youngjun Cho](https://profiles.ucl.ac.uk/54637-youngjun-cho), as part of the [UKRI Textile Circularity project](https://textilescircularity.rca.ac.uk/). During my PhD I also interned as a Data Scientist @Microsoft and as an AI research engineer @Autodesk Research.

Before my PhD, I worked in the aerospace industry✈️, where I designed and supervised the production of air turbine fan boosters and compressors—literally 🛫, not just a desk job!

Outside of work, I love food 🥘 and play Hearthstone Battlegrounds.
